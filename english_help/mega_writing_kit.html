বৃহৎ Phrase Bank + Transition Words (with example) + Vocabulary Bank (with example)

নোট: "৫ গুণ" ঠিক অতটা সংখ্যায় নিলে অনেক কৃত্রিম/পুনরাবৃত্তিমূলক এন্ট্রি ঢুকে যেত, তাই আমি প্রতিটা অংশ ~২-৩ গুণ বড় করেছি — সবই real, কাজে-লাগার মতো এন্ট্রি, আর Transition ও Vocabulary-তে এবার প্রতিটার সাথে Example Sentence যোগ করা হয়েছে যেমনটা চেয়েছিলেন।

সার্চ বক্সে টাইপ করে বা বাঁ পাশের তালিকা থেকে সরাসরি সেকশনে যান।

১. Phrase Bank — ৫২টা ফাংশন ক্যাটাগরি

যা করতে চান (request/apologize/inform/negotiate ইত্যাদি) সেটা খুঁজে নিন।

Opening
  • I am writing to [request/inform you about]...
  • I am writing in connection with...
  • Further to your email dated [date],...
  • With reference to our recent conversation,...
  • I hope this email finds you well.
  • I am writing on behalf of...
  • Thank you for your email regarding...
  • Following up on our discussion yesterday,...
Giving a Reason
  • This is due to [reason].
  • Owing to [reason],...
  • As a result of [event],...
  • In light of the recent [situation],...
  • Given the circumstances,...
  • The reason for this is that...
  • On account of [reason],...
Making a Request
  • I would be grateful if you could [action].
  • Could you please [action]?
  • I would like to request that [action].
  • Would it be possible to [action]?
  • I wonder if you could [action].
  • May I ask you to [action]?
  • I would appreciate it if you could [action].
Informing
  • Please be informed that [info].
  • This is to inform you that...
  • Kindly note that...
  • For your information,...
  • I am pleased to inform you that...
  • Please note that, effective [date],...
  • You may be interested to know that...
Apologizing
  • I apologize for any inconvenience caused.
  • Please accept my sincere apologies for [issue].
  • I regret to inform you that...
  • We sincerely apologize for the delay.
  • Please be assured that this will not happen again.
  • I take full responsibility for this oversight.
Complaining (Politely)
  • I am writing to express my concern about [issue].
  • I wish to raise an issue regarding...
  • Unfortunately, I have encountered a problem with...
  • This is not the level of service I expected.
  • I trust this matter will receive your immediate attention.
Proposing / Suggesting
  • I would like to propose that [idea].
  • May I suggest that...
  • It might be worth considering...
  • An alternative approach would be to...
  • I recommend that we...
  • Have you considered...?
Agreeing / Disagreeing
  • I fully agree that [point].
  • While I understand your point, I believe...
  • I am afraid I have to disagree, as...
  • I share your view on this matter.
  • To some extent, I agree, but...
Closing
  • I look forward to hearing from you.
  • Please do not hesitate to contact me.
  • Thank you for your time and consideration.
  • I would appreciate a prompt response.
  • Please feel free to reach out with any concerns.
  • I trust this meets your requirements.
Expressing Concern
  • I am somewhat concerned about [issue].
  • This raises a concern regarding...
  • I have some reservations about...
  • It is worth flagging that...
Giving Good News
  • I am delighted to inform you that...
  • I am pleased to announce that...
  • I am happy to confirm that...
  • Great news — [news].
Giving Bad News
  • I regret to inform you that...
  • Unfortunately, I must inform you that...
  • I am afraid that [news].
  • I am sorry to have to tell you that...
Making an Offer
  • We would like to offer you...
  • Should you require any assistance, please let us know.
  • We are pleased to extend an offer of...
  • Would you be interested in...?
Accepting
  • I am delighted to accept your offer of...
  • Thank you for the invitation; I would be happy to attend.
  • I gladly accept this opportunity.
Declining Politely
  • Thank you for the offer, but I must decline.
  • Unfortunately, I am unable to accept at this time.
  • I regret that I will not be able to attend.
Asking for Clarification
  • Could you please clarify what you mean by [term]?
  • Would you mind explaining this in more detail?
  • Just to confirm, are you saying that...?
Confirming Details
  • I am writing to confirm our appointment on [date].
  • This is to confirm that...
  • I can confirm that everything is in order.
Scheduling
  • Would [day/time] work for you?
  • I need to reschedule our meeting due to [reason].
  • Could we move the meeting to [new time]?
Following Up
  • I am following up on my previous email regarding...
  • Just a gentle reminder about...
  • Have you had a chance to look into this?
Expressing Gratitude
  • Thank you very much for your help with...
  • I truly appreciate your support in this matter.
  • Many thanks for taking the time to...
Congratulating
  • Congratulations on your [achievement]!
  • Well deserved — congratulations on this success.
Expressing Sympathy
  • I was deeply saddened to hear about...
  • My deepest condolences to you and your family.
Introducing Yourself
  • Allow me to introduce myself — I am [name].
  • We are a company specializing in...
  • I represent [company], and we would like to...
Attachments
  • Please find attached [document].
  • Kindly refer to the attached document for details.
Reminding
  • This is a gentle reminder that...
  • As previously mentioned,...
Formal Warning
  • Please be advised that failure to comply may result in...
  • This letter serves as formal notice that...
Summarizing
  • To summarize our discussion,...
  • The key points discussed were as follows:
Urgency
  • This matter requires immediate attention.
  • Please treat this as a matter of urgency.
Feedback
  • I would welcome your feedback on...
  • Overall, this was well done; one area to improve is...
Confidence / Uncertainty
  • I am confident that this approach will work.
  • It remains uncertain whether...
  • It is essential that...
Expressing Preference
  • I would prefer to [option A] rather than [option B].
  • My preference would be to...
  • Given the choice, I would opt for...
Expressing Regret
  • I regret that I was unable to...
  • In hindsight, it would have been better to...
  • I wish we had addressed this sooner.
Small Talk / Pleasantries
  • I hope you had a pleasant weekend.
  • I trust everything is going well on your end.
  • It was a pleasure meeting you last week.
Availability
  • I am available on [day] at [time].
  • I am free anytime after [time].
Unavailability
  • Unfortunately, I am unavailable on [date].
  • I will be out of office from [date] to [date].
Asking Permission
  • Would it be possible for me to [action]?
  • I would like to seek your approval for...
Giving Permission
  • You are welcome to proceed with...
  • Permission is hereby granted for...
Expressing Disappointment
  • I must admit I am disappointed with...
  • This falls short of what was agreed.
Expressing Relief
  • I am relieved to hear that...
  • It is a relief to know that the issue has been resolved.
Recommending
  • I highly recommend [person/service] for...
  • Without hesitation, I would suggest...
Expressing Hope
  • I am hopeful that this will lead to...
  • We remain optimistic about the outcome.
Expressing Opinion
  • In my view,...
  • I firmly believe that...
  • From my perspective,...
Comparing Options
  • Compared to [option], this offers better value.
  • Unlike the previous plan, this one is more flexible.
Explaining a Process
  • The process begins with [step], followed by...
  • Once [step] is complete, the next step is...
Giving Instructions
  • Please ensure that [action] before proceeding.
  • Kindly follow the steps outlined below.
Expressing Priority
  • This should take priority over...
  • The most pressing issue at hand is...
Announcing a Decision
  • After careful consideration, we have decided to...
  • We are pleased to announce our decision to...
Requesting an Extension
  • I would like to request an extension until [date].
  • Would it be possible to extend the deadline?
Requesting a Refund
  • I am writing to request a refund for...
  • Given the circumstances, I believe a refund is warranted.
Ending a Business Relationship
  • We regret to inform you that we will not be renewing...
  • After careful thought, we have decided to end our partnership.
Renewing an Agreement
  • We would like to renew our contract for another [period].
  • We are pleased with the partnership and wish to continue.
Expressing Interest
  • I am very interested in learning more about...
  • This opportunity aligns closely with my goals.
Welcoming Someone New
  • We are delighted to welcome [name] to the team.
  • Please join me in welcoming...
Wishing Well / Farewell
  • We wish you all the best in your future endeavors.
  • It has been a pleasure working with you.
Acknowledging, Confirming & Closing a Deal
  • This is to acknowledge receipt of [item].
  • Could you please confirm receipt of this email?
  • Please note that [constraint] applies.
  • As an alternative, we could consider...
  • We are pleased to finalize the agreement on these terms.
  • I am satisfied with the outcome of this arrangement.
